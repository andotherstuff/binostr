# Code of conduct

We want participation in this project to be safe, respectful, and constructive.

Be considerate in technical discussion, criticize ideas and evidence rather than people, respect differing experience and backgrounds, and do not engage in harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention, or publication of another person’s private information.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact mechanisms on the GitHub organization. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when representing the project in public spaces.
